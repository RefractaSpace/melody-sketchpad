6. 만 14세 미만

만 14세 미만은 회원가입을 받지 않습니다. 가입할 때 만 14세 이상인지 확인하며, 만 14세 미만이 가입한 사실을 알게 되면 그 계정과 정보를 지체 없이 지웁니다. 로그인 없이 쓰는 기능은 서버에 개인정보를 저장하지 않으므로 누구나 쓸 수 있습니다.

6. Children under 14

We don't accept accounts from children under 14. We ask you to confirm your age when you sign up, and if we learn that a child under 14 has an account, we delete it and its data without delay. Features that work without an account don't store personal data on our server and are open to everyone.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal data at any time.

  • In the app: Account window → view and delete your songs, change your password, delete account
  • By email: below0010@gmail.com — we reply within 10 days.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use cookies. We use browser storage (localStorage, IndexedDB) for your songs, settings, sign-in data (a signed token valid for 30 days) and a marker that today's visit was counted. Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it.

9. Security

  • Passwords are stored as one-way scrypt hashes with a unique salt per user
  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Sign-in is paused for 10 minutes after repeated wrong passwords
  • Admin functions are limited to the admin account
